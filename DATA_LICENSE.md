# Data provenance and reuse

This repository includes two **small subsets** of dissolved oxygen observations from the **Hawaii
Ocean Time-series (HOT)** program, exported through HOT-webODV:

| File | Source | Observation period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oxygen_bottle_hot_1999_2000.txt` | HOT via HOT-webODV | 1999–2000 |
| `oxygen_bottle_hot_2019_2020.txt` | HOT via HOT-webODV | 2019–2020 |

These files are excerpts selected for reproducing the example statistical analysis; they are not the
full HOT data collection. The original data are credited to the HOT program, not to the author of
this repository.

## Terms and attribution

The HOT program states that it provides free and open access to its observations, encourages
scientific use, requests acknowledgement, and asks data users to contact the program to avoid
duplication of efforts. Consult the original provider's current terms before redistributing or
reusing the subsets:

- HOT project and data access: https://hahana.soest.hawaii.edu/hot/dataaccess.html
- HOT-webODV service: https://hot.webodv.awi.de/data

For publications, follow HOT's current requested acknowledgement and cite the HOT observations and
HOT-webODV service appropriately. HOT-webODV gives the citation: Mieruch, S. & Schlitzer, R.,
HOT-webODV, https://hot.webodv.awi.de, 2026.
